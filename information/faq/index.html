Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common access and roleplay questions.

I cannot see any other channels.

Complete verification in QI verification. Without verification, other channels remain hidden.

Can I join Police, Ambulance, or Fire services?

Yes. Join the department Discord and complete its required training.

I'm unable to join the game.

You may be in a blacklisted group, there may be no active SSU, or training may be underway.

When are group requests and roles checked?

Requests and roles are checked randomly throughout the day and may not be reviewed before an SSU.

Can I get a firearm?

Only in a legitimate roleplay situation. Begging staff or using one for RDM will not help.
