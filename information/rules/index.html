Community Rules

Guidelines for Discord and Roblox.

Discord Guidelines

  1. Be Respectful: No harassment, bullying, discrimination, slurs, or targeted insults.
  2. No Earrape or Excessive Noise: Avoid loud, distorted, or disruptive audio.
  3. Use Common Sense: If it is disrespectful or disruptive, do not do it.
  4. No Spamming: Do not spam messages, emojis, reactions, pings, or notifications.
  5. Keep Content Appropriate: No NSFW, extreme violence, or disruptive arguments.
  6. Follow Discord's Terms: Follow Discord's Terms and Community Guidelines.
  7. No Advertising: Advertising requires staff permission.
  8. No Impersonation: Do not impersonate staff, officials, police, or members.
  9. Use Channels Properly: Post content in the correct channel.
  10. Listen to Staff: Follow moderator instructions.
  11. No Drama or Rumours: Do not spread false information.
  12. Privacy and Safety: Never share personal information. Doxxing is a permanent ban.
  13. Do Not Bypass Automod: Filter or security bypass attempts may result in a mute.

Roblox Guidelines

  1. Be Respectful: Treat every player respectfully.
  2. No Disruptive Audio: No sound spam, mic abuse, or disruptive effects.
  3. Use Common Sense: Exploiting loopholes is still a violation.
  4. No Spamming: No spam chat, commands, emotes, tools, vehicles, or animations.
  5. Keep Content Appropriate: No NSFW, extreme violence, or disruptive arguments.
  6. Follow Roblox Terms: Follow Roblox Terms and Community Standards.
  7. No Advertising: Other games, groups, servers, or products need permission.
  8. No Impersonation: Do not impersonate staff, officials, or players.
  9. No Unfair Advantages: Exploiting, hacking, glitch abuse, and third-party software are prohibited.
  10. Follow Staff Instructions: Comply with moderators and staff.
  11. No Drama or Trolling: Do not provoke arguments or spread rumours.
  12. Privacy and Safety: Never share personal information.
  13. Do Not Bypass Security: Do not bypass filters, moderation, or anti-exploit systems.
